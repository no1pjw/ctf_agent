# Forensics and misc reference

Work on copies, preserve originals, timestamps, and metadata, and record every transformation without calculating checksums. Inventory layers before extraction: archive, filesystem, packet, memory, media, document, log, model, or custom encoding.

Build a timeline/evidence graph and correlate independent sources. Treat macros, scripts, executables, links, and models as active content. OSINT work must not contact people or access non-public accounts.
